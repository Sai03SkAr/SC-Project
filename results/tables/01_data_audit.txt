FAILED LOOKUPS CARRY SIGNAL in these columns:
  ttl_hostname                 6.2% of rows fail, and those rows are 0.49x more likely to be legitimate
  qty_ip_resolved              6.2% of rows fail, and those rows are 0.50x more likely to be legitimate

  => `-1` is informative, not just noise. Tree models will exploit
     this correctly. Distance-based models (SVM, KNN) will NOT: they
     read -1 as 'slightly less than zero days old', placing failed
     lookups next to brand-new domains. Handle before using those.
